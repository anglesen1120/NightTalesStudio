## Design

### Differentiation contract (what makes this topic its own preset)

| Axis | ghost (T1) | **spiritual (this task)** |
|---|---|---|
| Source of fear | an unresolved presence | **consequence**: a debt, a promise, a wish with a price |
| Agency | the ghost acts | **the living** acts and must answer for it |
| Resolution | the image resolves, the metaphysics does not | **a choice** is made and its cost is paid (or refused) |
| Ending | lingering wrongness | moral closure *or* the cost arriving later |
| Taboo | explaining the ghost | preaching, moralising in the narrator's voice — the story shows the price, it does not sermonise |
| CTA | "have you heard something like this?" | "what would you have done?" / "Bạn sẽ chọn thế nào?" |

- Both presets keep the D3 shape; only the beat *content* rules differ. The beat **map** (hook → setup → build_up → clue → twist → reveal → aftermath → ending) is identical, so the validator and the review prompt need no per-topic branch.

### Section order (same 10 sections as T1)

Front-matter `id: spiritual.vi|en`, `topic: spiritual`, `language: vi|en`, `version: v1`, `role: preset`, `target_tier: all`. Sections: persona · beat map · hook rules · atmosphere rules · twist rules · ending + CTA · keywords · structure tags · length · output.

- **Persona** vi: a quiet narrator who speaks of the unseen as an ordinary fact of life, never as a preacher and never as a sceptic performing disbelief; en: same register.
- **Beat rules specific to this topic:** the `clue` must be a **promise, debt, gift or wish** the protagonist already accepted (so the twist is earned); the `reveal` shows who pays, not who is guilty; `aftermath` shows the cost in an ordinary detail (an empty chair, a returned object, a debt paid in full).
- **Length floor (02 T93):** the length section tells the model to reach at least the lower bound of `token_range` and `segment_range` before ending, and never to pad with filler: add a real scene beat instead, still inside the tier band.
- **Tone rules:** atmosphere from ritual detail, silence and family duty; **no gore**, **no sexual content**, **no minors in danger on screen** (02 T73: never show anyone under eighteen, named or unnamed, in a scene of harm or threat; a minor's danger only as a brief aftermath the narrator did not see); no real religion presented as a mechanism (no doctrinal claims, no ritual instructions that read as how-to).
- **Ending + CTA:** the last line lands the cost; CTA is a choice question, not a share request.
- **Keywords:** 1–2 per segment, English, visual scene, no person names/likeness; ritual objects are fine (incense, altar, lantern, river), real named temples/shrines are not.
- **Structure tags:** from the spiritual vocabulary (`nhân quả`, `luật âm dương`, `miếu nhỏ ven đường`, …); `other:<text>` fallback applies.
- **Output:** exactly `story.v1`; `fact_refs` = `[]`.

## Failure handling and edge cases

| Case | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Output preaches ("bài học là…") | the tone section is too weak: add an explicit "never state the lesson" rule and a bad example showing it |
| Output becomes doctrinal (names a real teaching/ritual as instruction) | forbidden by the tone rules; if the model persists, add the prohibition to the preset's atmosphere section with a bad example |
| Output drifts into ghost territory | the clue/agency rules are the fix (clue = accepted promise/debt/wish) |
| Brief has no karmic hook | the preset must still produce a story: use the `setting`/`opening` vocabulary to invent the debt in the brief's world; never refuse the brief |
| Output is valid but too long/short | tier band is data; if drift is systematic, raise F07 with the measured rate (never pad the preset) |
| Rollback | revert the two files + manifest entries |
