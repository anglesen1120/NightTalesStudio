# 08.3.2-T1 — Feedback loop: interview script and backlog themes

| Story | Size | Area | Depends on | Blocks | Phase / flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08.3.2 | S (0.5) | BA | 08.3.1-T1 | — | S · M2 |

## Goal

Turn tickets, monthly user interviews and usage data into backlog items with counts and evidence, reviewed weekly, so the roadmap follows what users actually hit (FR-08-4).

## Inputs

- `../../../08-90-day-operations.md` §4 (08.3.2: tickets, interviews — 5 beta users per month — and usage data; each theme becomes a backlog item with count and evidence; top items reviewed weekly).
- 08.3.1-T1's SLA log and tags; 08.1.2-T1's weekly template (`## Top 3 backlog changes`).
- 08.1.1-T1's KPI snapshot (usage data: activation, retention, render failures).
- `../../../13-compliance.md` §5.1 (staff access, retention) and the deletion rules: interview notes are pseudonymous, ids only, no story content.

## Design

**Backlog file.** `docs/ops/backlog.md` — one row per item: `id | theme | source (ticket | interview | usage) | count | evidence | first_seen | week_added | last_reviewed | decision (build | watch | decline) | reason`. `count` is the number of distinct occurrences (tickets, interviews, or the usage number) in the observation window; `evidence` links the ticket ids, the interview file and a KPI key or a query. The `decline` decision always carries a reason; nothing is dropped silently.

**Interview script.** `docs/ops/interviews/script.vi.md` (+ `script.en.md`): 8 open questions over ≤ 30 minutes — what did you try to do, where did you get stuck, what did you do instead, what made you trust/distrust the output, what would you pay for, what would make you stop, who else would you recommend it to, may we quote you anonymously. Rules: no leading questions, never ask for content, no recording without the consent line, notes pseudonymous (user id only). One record per interview: `docs/ops/interviews/YYYY-MM-<userid-short>.md` with the answers paraphrased and the consent line.

**Cadence.** Five interviews per month, chosen from users active in the last 30 days (from the admin list and the KPI snapshot), invited by e-mail from the owner's mailbox using a documented template; no incentive is promised without an owner decision.

**Review.** The weekly review (08.1.2-T1) reads the backlog, promotes the top three items, and notes `watch` items; monthly, the counts are refreshed from the SLA log and the snapshot.

## Steps

1. Create `docs/ops/backlog.md` with the columns above and seed it with the first themes from the SLA log (one row per theme, with counts).
2. Write `docs/ops/interviews/script.vi.md` and `script.en.md`, including the consent line and the pseudonymity rule.
3. Create the invitation template and pick the first five users from the active list; send the invitations from the owner's mailbox.
4. Run the first round, write one record per interview, and add the resulting themes to the backlog with counts.
5. Add the backlog section to the weekly template's `## Top 3 backlog changes` flow (link both ways) and do the first weekly triage.

## Outputs

- `docs/ops/backlog.md` (≥ 5 seeded themes with counts and evidence after the first round).
- `docs/ops/interviews/script.vi.md`, `script.en.md` and the first interview records.
- The invitation template and the first round's schedule.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] `docs/ops/backlog.md` exists with the ten columns, ≥ 5 themes, and every row has `count` + `evidence`.
- [ ] The script exists in vi and en with the consent line and the pseudonymity rule; no question asks for content or credentials.
- [ ] Five interviews are scheduled or completed in the first month, each with a record file; the schedule is visible in the weekly review.
- [ ] The weekly review's top-three section is filled from the backlog, and each `decline` has a reason.
- [ ] Every theme traces to at least one ticket id, interview file or KPI key.
- [ ] No personal data beyond a user id appears in any record.

## Tests

- Manual review: a reviewer reads the script for leading questions and the records for consent lines and pseudonymity.
- Consistency: for three sampled rows, the `count` matches the linked tickets/interviews/KPI window.
- No automated test: this is a BA artifact; a count-parity CI test would be source-text testing.

## Failure handling and edge cases

- Nobody accepts the invitation → record it, pick a different segment next month, and count the non-response rate as evidence in the backlog.
- A user asks for something out of scope (e.g. a feature owned by another plan) → `watch` with the reason and the owning plan named.
- A theme with one occurrence only → `watch`, never `build`, until the count justifies it.
- A user withdraws consent → delete that record and remove the theme's evidence link; keep the anonymised count.
- Two conflicting themes → both stay, each with its own count and the affected segment named.

## Notes

- FR-08-4 is satisfied by the backlog plus the review loop; do not build a feedback table or a survey tool.
- The counts are the argument; a theme without a count is an opinion and stays out of the top three.
- This loop is also the input for Gate 2's evidence and the day-30/60 decisions (D13).
