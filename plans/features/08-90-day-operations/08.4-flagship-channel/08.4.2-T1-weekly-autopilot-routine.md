# 08.4.2-T1 — Weekly autopilot operations routine: veto review, sample audit, time log, incident notes

| Story | Size | Area | Depends on | Blocks | Phase / flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08.4.2 | M (1.0) | BA/Ops | 08.4.1-T1, 02.8.3-T4, 06.2.2-T3 | 08.4.3-T1 | P · Channel phase (recurring; estimate covers setup) |

## Goal

Turn the Channel phase into a repeatable 45-minute weekly routine that **operates** the autopilot instead of producing episodes (F1/D2), and leave one file per week — `docs/ops/flagship/weekly/YYYY-WW.md` — filled from a template shipped by this task. The routine's output feeds 08.4.3-T1's KPI row and the Gate 2 evidence (owner touch time per episode is a Gate 2 input, K1/F5).

Deliverable is a template plus one rehearsal week filled from real (staging or rehearsal) data: no code, no route, no job, no new metric.

## Inputs

- `docs/ops/flagship/setup.md` and `calendar.md` (08.4.1-T1) — the accepted authorization (`version`, `textHash`), the settings, the week's planned slots, the audit/gates state.
- 02.8.3-T4 — pause mechanics: `autopilot_paused_at` + `autopilot_pause_reason`, the 15 reason codes, the alert e-mail naming already-scheduled videos, resume re-checking the triggers.
- 06.2.2-T3 — `AutopilotConsentJob`: the veto notice, the veto window (`veto_window_hours`), and the consent record (`consent_at`, `consent_text_version`, `consent_source = autopilot`); a notice never delivered (`veto_notice_sent_at` empty) pauses the series with `veto_notice_undelivered`.
- 06.2.2-T1/T2 — the veto notice/cancel link surface (publication edit during the window; the cancel-link token kind `publication_cancel` minted by 06.2.2-T2).
- 02.8.2 `plan.md` §3.1/§3.4 + D16 — `story_versions.audit_report` (`audit.v1`: `model_review.scores` over the seven dimensions, `issues[]`, `similarity`) and `autopilot_runs.gate_results` (`gates.v1`: `checks[]` with `id/value/threshold/pass/detail`, `failures[]`).
- `GET /series/{id}/autopilot` + `GET /series/{id}/autopilot/runs` (02.8.3-T2) — the live state and the run log for the week.
- 11.6.2-T1 alert rules 5, 10–12, 15–17 — the paging surface to read (K9); no new alert is configured (D5).
- `../README.md` D2 (the template's field list), K9/K10, F1/F5.

## Design

**1. The template `docs/ops/flagship/weekly/_template.md`** — one file per ISO week, sections in this fixed order (each prefixed with its time box so the routine is clockable):

```markdown
# <YYYY-Www> — flagship week
Week: <Mon–Sun dates> · Planned slots: <3 dates from calendar.md> · Actual published: <n>

### 1. Veto review (10 min)
| Slot | Episode/Publication | Verdict (approved untouched / edited / cancelled) | What changed | Time (min) |
Authorization version/textHash in force: <from setup.md>

### 2. Sample audit (20 min) — one published video, watched in full
Sampled: <episode id, slot date, publication id>
- File/video checks: resolution, duration, audio, subtitles present? <ok | deviation>
- Rubric (same anchors as the model review, D16), 1–5 each:
  hook · pacing · atmosphere · natural language · originality · factual care (n/a for fiction) · length fit
  owner: <…> · model mean from audit.v1: <…> · divergence > 1.0 on any dimension? <no | dimension + why>
- Script vs narration: any silent edit, mispronunciation, wrong number, wrong place name? <none | list>
- Gate record for this episode: story checks failed <ids or none>; video checks failed <ids or none>
Issues worth a settings change (tone / banned_themes / cadence / tier): <none | list>

### 3. True crime and owner approvals (within the slot review above)
True-crime episodes held for me: <episode id, decision, time (min)>
Gate failures waiting for me in story_review / video_review: <episode id, cause, decision, time (min)>

### 4. Pauses, alerts, incidents (10 min)
Pauses this week: <reason code from the 02.8.3-T4 enum, run/slot, cause, resumed? when, by whom>
Alerts received (11.6.2-T1 rules): <rule #, what, action>
Platform incidents: <none | platform_incidents row id, error_code, cleared?>
Veto notices not delivered / undelivered-consent pauses: <none | slot + state>
Publications still awaiting consent at week end: <list>
Pre-audit private count: <n> (mode: keep_cadence_private)

### 5. Owner touch time and decisions (5 min)
Owner touch minutes this week: veto <..> + sample audit <..> + approvals <..> + incidents <..> = <total>
Episodes published this week: <n> → touch minutes per episode: <total / n>
Decisions for next week: <…>
Settings/authorization changes made (each covered-field change revokes → re-accepted?): <none | list>
```

Constraints baked into the template: a field with nothing to report is written `none` (never left blank, so an empty section is distinguishable from a forgotten one); every time cell is minutes spent *this week*, not an estimate; the authorization line at the top of §1 is the anchor that proves the consent texts matched the accepted version (K7).

**2. The routine (UC-2).** Order matters so the veto window is respected: read the week's veto notices first, then the run log/state, then the sample audit, then log and decide.

1. **Veto review (10 min).** For each autopilot publication that entered its veto window this week (the notice e-mails, cross-checked against `GET /series/{id}/autopilot/runs` `waitingFor` and publication state): if the script/metadata is acceptable, do nothing (the window expires and `AutopilotConsentJob` writes the consent with `consent_source = autopilot`). If not, either edit inside the window (an edit inside the window is an implicit consent, 02.8.3-T2/06.2.2) or cancel the publication. Record every veto **seen**, not only every veto **acted on** — the "seen/edited/cancelled" counts are the D2 evidence that the window is real work.
2. **Sample audit (20 min).** Watch **one** published video of the week in full and read its script; score the seven D16 dimensions; compare against the model's mean in `audit.v1`; check the gate record. One video per week is the D2 minimum — a week with four published videos still samples **one**, chosen deliberately (prefer the week's least confident output: a slot that needed an extra attempt, or a topic closest to a borderline gate check).
3. **Approvals.** True-crime episodes and everything the gates held go through the normal review screens; record decision + minutes. If the gates are left in `DryRun`, fiction approvals also live here — annotate §3 with `gates dry run` so the week's touch time is not mistaken for the steady-state number.
4. **Pauses, alerts, incidents (10 min).** Read the alert channel (11.6.2-T1 rules 5, 10–12, 15–17) and the Autopilot tab; for every pause record the reason code verbatim from the 02.8.3-T4 enum, the cause and the resume decision. A pause is not resolved by re-enabling: the trigger must be cleared (spend cap, incident, quota, notice delivery, channel status).
5. **Log (5 min).** Fill the template, compute touch minutes per episode, write next week's decisions, and hand the week's numbers to 08.4.3-T1.

**3. Time budget.** 10 + 20 + 10 + 5 = **45 min/week** (D2). The budget is the routine's acceptance, not a target to beat: a week that takes 90 min is a finding (record the overrun and its cause — a pause storm, an undelivered notice, manual editing after a gate failure), not a moral failure.

**4. What this routine must not do.** No manual episode production; no script rewriting outside true crime and gate failures; no direct Studio privacy changes except in the takedown runbook (08.4.4-T1); no analytics API; no new alert.

## Steps

1. Write `docs/ops/flagship/weekly/_template.md` exactly as §Design 1, with the six sections in order and the time boxes in the headings.
2. Rehearse once on a completed rehearsal week (the private pre-audit week from 08.4.1-T1 §11.3): pull the week's runs, one veto notice (or its absence), the sample video and its `audit.v1`, and fill every field.
3. Time the rehearsal with a clock; if it exceeds 45 min, record where the time went in §5 and trim the routine (e.g. sample the shorter video, pre-filter the run log) rather than dropping a section.
4. Verify each cross-check the template claims is actually available: `audit.v1` scores readable for the sampled episode; `gates.v1` failures readable; the pause reason code visible in the Autopilot tab; the consent source visible; the touch time derivable.
5. Exercise one veto path end to end on staging: pick a publication inside its window, make one legitimate edit (e.g. a title typo), confirm the implicit consent and that no extra consent row is created with `consent_source = user` where autopilot was expected — record the observation in the rehearsal week's file.
6. Exercise the cancel path once: use the notice's cancel link (`publication_cancel` token) on a staging publication that should not publish, confirm the publication ends `cancelled` and the slot's run outcome reflects it.
7. Write the routine's operating notes at the bottom of `_template.md` (a short "how to fill this" block: where each field comes from) so a later operator can run it without re-deriving the sources.
8. Hand the first filled week to 08.4.3-T1 as its input.

## Outputs

- `docs/ops/flagship/weekly/_template.md` (new).
- One filled `docs/ops/flagship/weekly/YYYY-WW.md` for the rehearsal week (the pattern 08.4.3-T1 will consume weekly).
- No repository file outside `docs/ops/flagship/`; no code, route, migration, metric or alert added.

## Tests

BA dry runs/rehearsals (no xunit/`PostgresFixture`):

1. **V5 template fillable in the box** — fill the rehearsal week from scratch against real data; clock it; every field filled or `none`; ≤ 45 min. This is the routine's main proof.
2. **Veto path rehearsal** — one legitimate edit inside a window produces an implicit consent that publishes; assert the consent's `consent_source = autopilot`, its `consent_text_version = YouTubeOptions.ConsentTextVersion` (`pc.v1` — **not** the authorization's `textHash`, 06.2.2-T3:183) and its `autopilot_authorization_id` equal to the authorization accepted in 08.4.1-T1 (its `version` recorded in `setup.md`), and that the publication still went out at `publish_at`.
3. **Cancel path rehearsal** — the cancel link cancels the publication and the slot's run outcome records the cancellation; record both in the week's file.
4. **Undelivered-notice path** — with a staging publication whose `veto_notice_sent_at` is empty, confirm the series pauses with `veto_notice_undelivered` (trigger 11) and that the weekly file's §4 can name it; resume after clearing.
5. **Time-source check** — recompute the week's touch minutes a second time from the raw inputs (e-mail timestamps + the run log) and compare with §5; a difference means a missed activity.
6. **Divergence-scoring reproducibility** — re-score the sampled video's `hook` and `length fit` from the same notes a day later: the two scores must be within one point, proving the §2 scores are usable as Gate 2 evidence.

## Failure handling and edge cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| No slot ran this week (cadence gap/holiday) | §1–§3 = `none`, §4 records why (paused? cadence? no run created), §5 records 0 published; do not skip the file — a missing week breaks the week sequence the checkpoints read. |
| All output is `private` (pre-audit rehearsal) | Fill the file normally and mark §4's pre-audit count; the week does **not** count toward the 12 public weeks (F4). |
| Veto notice never arrives | Trigger 11 pauses the series (K10). §4 records `veto_notice_undelivered`; check the e-mail channel/`email_messages` status, then resume only after the notice path is proven — otherwise every future window is a blind auto-consent. |
| Owner is away longer than the veto window | Auto-consent is the documented behaviour (K2), so **disable** autopilot before travelling (or set a longer window) and record the decision; do not rely on catching notices late. |
| A gate failure leaves an episode in `story_review` | Manual editing is allowed here (D2 exception). Record it in §3 with the failed check ids; if the same check id fails twice in a week, §5's decision is a settings/threshold change (02.8.2-T3) rather than more editing. |
| Same reason code pauses twice in a week | Record both; if it is `spend_cap_reached` or `provider_funds_exhausted`, §5 records the cost decision (the KPI sheet will show it too) — repeated pauses are a Gate 2 input, not a routine nuisance. |
| Touch time over the box for 2 consecutive weeks | §5 records the overrun and the top time consumer; the decision is either a settings change (fewer veto edits by improving `tone`/`banned_themes`) or accepting a higher steady-state number as Gate 2 evidence. |
| A pause spans a veto window | `AutopilotConsentJob` skips a publication whose series is paused (the 02 CR landed in 06.2.2-T3:64,155), so those rows stay `awaiting_consent` and their upload is late by the pause length — they do **not** silently auto-consent while paused. Record the delayed count in §4 and re-check them at resume. |
| `publish_at` slips or a publication ends `failed` | §4 records `upload_failed`/`youtube_quota_exhausted` and whether it retried (`scheduled` + `next_attempt_at`); the slot count in §5 uses **published** videos, not planned slots. |
| The owner wants to change the cadence mid-week | Apply through the product after the week is closed and record the amendment in §5; a covered-field change revokes the authorization (K7) and must be re-accepted before the next slot. |

## Notes

- This task's M (1.0) estimate covers the template, the rehearsal, the two path rehearsals and the operating notes; the weekly routine afterwards is recurring work measured in `kpi.md`, not re-estimated.
- A weekly file that cannot be filled in 45 min is a product finding (F1's point: the routine is the UX instrument) — record it in §5 rather than hiding it by skipping the audit.
- The sample audit is deliberately **one** video (D2). Sampling more would improve the sample but break the time box; the sample's coverage over 12 weeks (~12 videos) is what the checkpoints use.
