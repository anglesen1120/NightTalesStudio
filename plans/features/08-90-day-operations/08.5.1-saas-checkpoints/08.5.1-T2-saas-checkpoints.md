# 08.5.1-T2 — SaaS 30/60/90 checkpoint criteria and reminders

| Story | Size | Area | Depends on | Blocks | Phase / flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Goal

`docs/ops/saas-checkpoints.md`: the day-30/60/90 go/no-go rows with `metric key | window | target | source | decision rule`, signed by the owner, plus the reminder rule — so stopping or doubling down is a deliberate, dated decision on numbers that already have names (D13, F12).

## Inputs

- `../../../08-90-day-operations.md` §4 (08.5.1's day-30/60/90 text) and §5.1 (targets).
- §B3 D7 (the KPI keys and their sources) and D13 (the document's shape and the reminder rule).
- `../../08.5-checkpoints/08.5.1-T1-gate2-checkpoints.md` and `docs/ops/gate-2.md` (the P precedent for the format and the Gate 2 precondition).
- `GET /admin/funding` (12.8.1-T1) for 7/30-day revenue and provider cost; 12.5.2-T1 for margin; 11.6.3-T1's cost-per-video measurement for context.
- 08.1.2-T1's weekly template (the countdown row) and 08.1.1-T1's snapshot.

## Design

**File.** `docs/ops/saas-checkpoints.md`: §0 header + owner sign-off (date, name, Gate 2 decision reference), §1 the three checkpoint rows, §2 the KPI key list it reads (copied by name from D7), §3 the reminder rule, §4 the revision log.

**Row shape.** `day | metric key | window | target | source | decision rule | status | decision`. Sources: `kpi` (the D7 key, with its `window`), `funding` (`GET /admin/funding`), `manual` (`infra_cost_30d_usd`).

**The three checkpoints.**
- Day 30 — `render_success_rate ≥ 95 %`, `deletion_on_time_rate = 100 %`, `margin_percent ≥ 60 %`, plus "no data-duty misses" → decision rule: all met → open sign-ups wider; any missed → hold sign-ups, fix first, re-check at day 45.
- Day 60 — `second_episode_within_30d_rate ≥ 30 %` (of activated users) → met → start paid acquisition tests (a budget decision, taken separately); missed → no paid spend, find the blocker in the backlog.
- Day 90 — revenue (7/30-day from `/admin/funding`) ≥ provider cost (same source) + `infra_cost_30d_usd` (manual: node + storage + telemetry invoice lines, itemised in §1) → met → continue; missed → revisit pricing/segment (12.4/12.7 inputs), recorded with the owner's decision.

**Reminder rule.** A standing item in 08.1.2-T1's weekly review shows "next checkpoint in N days" plus the three checkpoint dates; the owner also puts them in their own calendar. No job, no e-mail template (D13 — deliberately not a platform feature).

**Revision.** After signing, a target change requires a dated revision row naming the old and new value and the owner's initials, and is announced in the weekly notes.

## Steps

1. Draft §0–§4 with the three rows, the sources and the decision rules above.
2. Fill §2 with the exact D7 key names and the one manual line; verify each key exists in 08.1.1-T1's `KpiTargets`.
3. Add the countdown row to `docs/ops/weekly/TEMPLATE.md` (08.1.2-T1) and the owner's calendar entries with the three dates.
4. Get the owner's sign-off on §0 and record it; if Gate 2 is recorded as no-go, mark the rows `not scheduled` and stop.

## Outputs

- `docs/ops/saas-checkpoints.md` with the sign-off block.
- The countdown row in the weekly template and the calendar entries.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] The document has §0–§4 with a signed header and three rows, each with all six columns.
- [ ] Every metric key in §1/§2 exists in 08.1.1-T1's `KpiTargets`, except the explicit `infra_cost_30d_usd` manual line, which is itemised.
- [ ] The day-90 revenue and provider-cost entries name `GET /admin/funding`; margin names 12.5.2-T1.
- [ ] The reminder rule is the weekly countdown row + the owner's calendar, with no new job or template.
- [ ] The weekly template contains the countdown line and the three dates.
- [ ] No number in the document is an estimate presented as a measurement.

## Tests

- Review (acceptance): the key names are compared with §B3 D7 and `KpiTargets`; the row shape is compared with the P precedent `docs/ops/gate-2.md`.
- Manual: the day-30 checkpoint is performed on or after day 30 with real numbers, and the decision is written in §1 and reflected in the weekly notes.
- Negative: a missing key is recorded as a data gap with an owner and a date, and the row stays `unknown` until it is filled.
- No automated test is added (document-only task), consistent with 08.5.1-T1.

## Failure handling and edge cases

- Gate 2 = no-go → the rows are `not scheduled` and the reason is recorded in §0; the document stays as the record.
- A metric key is renamed later (08.1.1-T1 contract change) → a revision row renames it here in the same pull request; the countdown row keeps working.
- The infra cost lines are not yet invoiced at day 90 → record the last full month and state the lag; never substitute an estimate for a measurement.
- Fewer than 30 days of data at day 30 (late launch) → shift the checkpoint by the recorded launch delay, not by silently bending the window.
- The owner wants to change a target mid-flight → the revision rule above, with the reason in the weekly notes.
