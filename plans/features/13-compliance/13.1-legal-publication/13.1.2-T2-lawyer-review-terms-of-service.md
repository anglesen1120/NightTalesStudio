## Goal

Deliver the **lawyer-reviewed terms of service as the published version**: the external review of 13.1.2-T1's draft `terms.1.0.0.vi.md` / `.en.md`, the clause-by-clause dispositions applied to **both** locales, the version decided by the materiality checklist (folder `plan.md` §3.3), the manifest `tos` entry kept in step, and the publish through 13.2.2-T1's operator verb (`D6`, `--doc tos`). The ToS is the document that carries the YouTube **III.A.1** link (`https://www.youtube.com/t/terms`) and the binding sentence, the AI-disclosure duty, the "no monetisation guarantee", the statement that the user is the publisher of record, and the credit/refund clauses that must match plan 12 exactly. This task owns the review, the version decision and the publish; it does not own the verb (13.2.2-T1), the page renderer (10.3.1-T1) or the acceptance gate (Part A `13.2.1-T1`). External lawyer time is a **cost** line, not a dev-day line (`O4`).

## Design

**Review protocol.** The same clause matrix shape as 13.1.1-T2, sourced from plan §5.2 plus the four cross-plan rows (credit expiry, refund window, AI disclosure, publisher of record):

`| checklist item | source (§5.2 / III.A.1 / cross-plan) | draft paragraph | disposition accepted|reworded|rejected | reason | anchor in the new text |`

Each §5.2 row exists exactly once; a missing row or an empty reason on a `reworded`/`rejected` row fails `test_check_compliance_docs.py`.

**The ⚠️ items this task must NOT settle.** Governing law/disputes ⚠️, OpenAI terms on **output ownership** ⚠️ (plan §5.2, §10), credit expiry 12 months ⚠️ and the refund window ⚠️. The reviewer either confirms them (then the log records the confirmation and its basis) or the text ships a neutral formulation and the item stays ⚠️ **open** in the log and in 13.4.1-T1's filing notes. Silence is not a resolution, and "lawyer reviewed" must not appear in user copy while any of the three substantive items is unresolved.

**Version + materiality (§3.3).** Five rows (obligation / right / retention window / price-credit rule / data category). Any change to a credit, refund or liability clause is `price-credit rule = changed` → **minor** (`1.1.0`) + re-acceptance via 13.2.1-T2/D7. Wording-only fixes on confirmed clauses → **patch** or no change.

**Publish sequence (`D6`).**
1. Deploy the web artifact (`content_url` is a deployed static path, never external — `T6`).
2. `nts-worker legal publish --doc tos --version <v> --locale vi --effective-at <ISO> --content-url /content/legal/terms.<v>.vi.md`, then `--locale en`.
3. Verify the `legal.document_published` audit rows (`support`, `internal`) and that the manifest `tos` version equals the max `legal_documents` row for (kind `tos`, locale) (`D6b`).
4. A missing served file returns **409 `legal_content_missing`** and writes no row: deploy first, never hand-insert the row (the verb owns `published_by`/`checksum`, CR-7).

**Content obligations the review must confirm.** Eligibility 18+ (`D8`); account security; credits non-cash + expiry + refunds mirroring 12 verbatim in meaning; acceptable use linking the Content Policy (13.1.3-T1) instead of restating it; ownership of outputs subject to provider terms ⚠️; AI-disclosure duty; no monetisation guarantee; user is the publisher of record on their channels; platform-rule compliance (YouTube/TikTok); takedown/suspension; service availability; liability limits; governing law ⚠️.

**Hand-off, not an edit here.** `LegalDocuments:Current:Tos` must equal the published max row after the change (`D6b`); the startup check asserts it. resolved (folder `plan.md` §10 O-6/T15): the config is seed-only, no task updates it.

## Steps

1. Build the §5.2 clause matrix plus the cross-plan rows; send the draft, the matrix, plan 12's credit/refund wording and the III.A.1 requirement to the lawyer.
2. Record the dispositions in `docs/compliance/terms-review-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md` (reviewer identity/credentials, date, scope, the shared engagement with 13.1.1-T2 per `O4`).
3. Apply the rewordings to both locales; keep the credit/refund sentences semantically identical to plan 12's wording and do not add a promise 12 does not make.
4. Fill the materiality checklist; mint the version; record which of the ⚠️ items remain open.
5. Add the files at the new version, update the manifest `tos` key, run `tools/ci/check-legal-content.py` and `tools/ci/check-legal-versions.py` locally.
6. Deploy, publish both locales, capture the audit rows, the row versions/checksums and the timestamps.
7. If the review changes the credit/refund or liability clauses, notify 13.2.1-T2's owner (re-acceptance) and 13.3.2-T1's owner (the account-deletion refund path reads 12's rules; the ToS must not contradict them).
8. Log the ⚠️ remainder for 13.4.1-T1 (a filing with an unresolved governing-law clause is allowed; one with a false statement is not).

## Outputs

- Reviewed `web/src/content/legal/terms.<v>.vi.md` + `.en.md` and the updated `manifest.json` `tos` entry (or an unchanged `1.0.0` when nothing changed).
- `docs/compliance/terms-review-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md`: clause matrix, materiality checklist, version decision, the open ⚠️ list, publish evidence.
- One `legal_documents` row per locale (kind `tos`, version, locale, `effective_at`, `content_url`, `published_by`, `checksum`) plus the `legal.document_published` audit rows — or zero rows when nothing changed.
- Hand-offs: published ToS URL + version for 13.4.1-T1 (`D12` requires the ToS URL on a verified domain) and for 13.5.1-T1's evidence index.

## Acceptance checks

- **Given** the draft and the reviewer's dispositions, **when** `docs/compliance/terms-review-<date>.md` is complete, **then** every §5.2 row and the four cross-plan rows have a disposition, the III.A.1 link and binding sentence are marked present in both locales, and each open ⚠️ item is listed with its unresolved question.
- **Given** the reviewer changed the refund window, **when** the materiality checklist is filled, **then** `price-credit rule = changed` → version `1.1.0`, re-acceptance required, and the log names 13.2.1-T2.
- **Given** a deployed `terms.1.1.0.vi.md` + `.en.md`, **when** the publish verb runs for `vi` then `en`, **then** it exits 0, two rows exist, two audit entries `legal.document_published` exist, and `/vi/terms` + `/en/terms` show `1.1.0`.
- **Given** an undeployed version, **when** the verb runs, **then** it fails with 409 `legal_content_missing` and writes no row.
- **Given** a credit/refund sentence written from memory, **when** the review closes, **then** the sentence matches plan 12's rule (expiry, refund window, spent-bonus deduction) or the mismatch is a recorded backlog row — the ToS must not promise a refund 12 does not grant.
- **Given** a text promising a feature the product lacks (e.g. account export within 24 h), **when** the log is closed, **then** the promise is removed or a backlog row exists in the owning plan.
- **Given** the memo (13.6.1-T1) has not landed, **when** the ToS is published, **then** no sentence claims PDPL/other-law compliance (`D16`); the eligibility/children and governing-law items stay ⚠️ as recorded.

## Failure handling and edge cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| Lawyer unavailable / engagement slips | owner-only personal version stays live (`O1`); no publish, nothing claims a review |
| Reviewer cannot settle governing law or OpenAI output ownership | neutral wording ships; the item stays ⚠️ **open** in the log and in 13.4.1-T1's notes; never marked resolved by silence |
| Plan 12 changes its credit/refund rule later | CR-14/12's folder owns the change; this task's ToS text is re-issued at a new version and (being `price-credit rule = changed`) forces re-acceptance — record the trigger in the calendar rows of 13.5.2-T1 |
| One locale missing | not publishable (`D17`); locale parity is asserted by the content check |
| Served file 404 / not deployed | 409 `legal_content_missing`, no row; deploy and retry |
| Manifest/config stale | CI red at `check-legal-versions.py`; fix the value, never the check |
| Version bump is material | 13.2.1-T2 `pendingAcceptance`; the previous `tos` acceptance does not satisfy the new version (`D7`) |
| Wrong text published | supersede with a new version (no delete verb exists, `D6`); the old row documents what was accepted |
| The ToS must state a retention fact (30-day backups) | the fact comes from 11.6.1/plan 13 §5.1/§5.4, not from the ToS author; if 11 changes it, this text follows at a new version |
