# 13.6.1-T1 — Vietnamese data-protection review: memo + backlog

| Story | Size | Area | Depends on | Blocks | Phase / flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13.6.1 | S (0.5) | Legal | 13.1.1-T1, 13.1.2-T1 (soft) | 13.2.2-T1 (soft) | S · M2 |

## Goal

Replace the ⚠️ placeholder plan 13 §2 C6 still carries ("exact instrument, obligations and effective dates not verified") with a **lawyer-consulted memo plus an actionable backlog**: `docs/compliance/pdpd-memo-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md` names the instruments, lists the duties with the product or process change each implies, states plainly what is **not** met yet, and gives one backlog row per required change naming the owning plan/folder. The memo is the **only** place a Vietnamese-law compliance claim can be earned — until it lands, no user-facing text, audit answer or filing may assert one (`D16`). Where the memo requires a text change, the change goes through 13.2.2-T1's publish path (`D6`) with the version bump of folder `plan.md` §3.3 (hence the soft edge into 13.2.2-T1). External consultant time is a **cost** line, not a dev-day line (`O4`).

## Inputs

- **`D16` (verbatim) ✅** — instruments: **Law 91/2025/QH15** (in force 2026-01-01), **Decree 356/2025/ND-CP**, sanctions **Decree 330/2026/ND-CP**; **Decree 13/2023/ND-CP superseded**; duty list to cover: consent wording, notices, records of processing, impact-assessment dossier, cross-border transfer to OpenAI, breach notification, children, DPO/contact; "the **obligation list stays ⚠️** until the lawyer confirms it"; deliverable path pattern; one backlog row per required change; a text change means a version bump through `D6`.
- **`K16` ⚠️** — instrument numbers/titles are ✅ against the cited gazette pages, but the **dates** (enacted 2025-06-26 / in force 2026-01-01) rest on secondary sources and the gazette pages expose metadata, not the date text: 13.6.1-T1 **must not promote those dates to ✅** on this evidence. Decree 13/2023/ND-CP is the superseded instrument — a writer must not chase it.
- **Plan 13 §5.1/§5.2/§5.4 and 00 §11** — the retention/deletion facts the texts already state (7-day and 30-day windows, 30-day backup retention, the restore re-apply rule) so the memo compares law against the *stated* behaviour, not against an assumption.
- **Signed P contracts — not reopened (`T7`)**: `user_consents` + `IConsentWriter.RecordAsync`, the `ILegalAcceptance` gate (Part A D3), `LegalDocuments:Current:*`, `data_deletion_requests` (Part A D4). A duty that appears to conflict is raised as a CR, never as a task-level redesign.
- **`D8`** — 18+ self-attestation via `user_consents.document = 'age_18'` (CR-11), no identity verification in S; the memo checks that against the children duties ⚠️.
- **`D11`/`D13`/`D9`/`D10`** — the other S surfaces the memo must assess: the public report path, the cookie inventory, the DSAR export and account deletion.
- **`D14`** — the memo is a text artifact; no personal data, no secret; redaction grep before 13.5.1-T1 cites it.

## Design

**Memo structure (mandatory sections — enforced by `tests/ci/test_check_compliance_docs.py`).**

| § | Section | Required content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instruments | the four names verbatim with their status: numbers/titles ✅ (K16), dates ⚠️ with the note that they come from secondary sources |
| 2 | Scope and assumptions | what was reviewed (texts, flows, data map, sub-processors), what was shown to the consultant, what was out of scope |
| 3 | Duties table | columns `duty, source (instrument/section ⚠️), current state, gap, required change, owner plan/folder` — one row per duty, including the eight `D16` duty families |
| 4 | Cross-border transfer | OpenAI (story/image generation) and any other non-VN processor: the transfer basis, the notice/consent wording, the dossier duty ⚠️, and which text states it |
| 5 | Records of processing and impact assessment | whether a processing register and/or an impact-assessment dossier is required ⚠️, who maintains it, where it lives, and the retention of those documents |
| 6 | Breach notification | the notification duty and window ⚠️, who decides, and the existing incident path (`08 §5.2`/08.3 runbook) it attaches to — no new mechanism invented |
| 7 | Children | the 18+ gate (`D8`) as the control; the child-data duties ⚠️ and what the age self-attestation can and cannot prove |
| 8 | Contact / DPO | the statutory contact duty ⚠️ and whether an appointment is required; the contact published in the Privacy Policy and the ToS |
| 9 | What is not met yet | the honest list, each item carrying its §3 row id; no item may be softened ("partially aligned" is not "met") |
| 10 | Backlog | one row per required change, columns `id, change, owner plan/folder, size guess, trigger/deadline, status` — these rows become work in the owning plans (13 §9 exit: closed by M3; **no M3 task is created in this folder**, per README §B8.1's note) |
| 11 | Engagement record | consultant identity/credentials, date, scope, fee agreed, and what was explicitly not covered |
| 12 | Open ⚠️ questions | the lawyer's unresolved questions with what would settle each (document, filing, decision) |

**Rules the checker enforces.**
1. Every duty row has a non-empty `gap` OR an explicit `none` with a one-line justification; a blank cell fails.
2. Every §3 row with a non-empty `required change` has a matching §10 backlog row (referenced by row id), and every §10 row names an owner plan/folder that exists in this repo.
3. The four instrument names appear verbatim; the string `Decree 13/2023/ND-CP` appears **only** in the "superseded" statement (never as the applicable instrument).
4. No `✅` may be adjacent to a date of the instruments (K16's trap), and the forbidden-claims list (`compliant`, `fully compliant`, `in compliance with`) must not appear anywhere in the memo or in any text artifact of this folder before the memo exists — the same `forbidden-claims` grep used by 13.1.x-T2's review logs.

**Handling a required text change.** If a duty needs different wording in the Privacy Policy, the ToS, the Content Policy or the cookie notice, §10 carries an item of the form "re-issue <doc> at the next version" and the change is published through 13.2.2-T1 (`D6`) with the materiality test of folder `plan.md` §3.3 — a material rewrite forces re-acceptance via 13.2.1-T2/`D7`, and the version must be minted on the **reviewed** text (so a memo-driven change ideally lands before or with the 13.1.x-T2 publish — the soft edge into 13.2.2-T1).

**Evidence hygiene.** The memo holds no user e-mail, IP, token, tenant row or story text; examples use the synthetic tenant convention (`demo+<n>@invalid`) if one is needed at all. It is stored in-repo as text; any consultant annex with personal data stays outside the repo (`~/.nts/compliance/`, `D14`) and only its reference + sha256 appears in the memo.

## Steps

1. Assemble the fact pack handed to the consultant: the four texts (Privacy Policy, ToS, Content Policy, cookie inventory), the data map (tables/objects/processors incl. OpenAI), the deletion/retention facts (7/30 days, 30-day backups, restore re-apply), the 18+ gate, the public report path, the DSAR export and account deletion, the DPO/contact situation.
2. Run the engagement (shared with nothing else in this folder; the lawyer fee line is `O4`'s pattern) and record §11.
3. Write §1 (verbatim names, ⚠️ dates), §2, and §3 with one row per duty family.
4. For each gap, decide the required change and its owner plan/folder; write §10 with the same ids.
5. Write §4–§8 (transfer, records/impact assessment, breach, children, contact/DPO) with the current state named from repo evidence, never from memory.
6. Write §9 honestly and §12 with the open questions and what would settle each.
7. Run `tools/ci/check-compliance-docs.py` (all four rules) and fix what it flags.
8. Hand each §10 row to its owning plan/folder (a message to that folder's owner + a CR line where the README's CR table applies, e.g. CR-9 to `plans/13-compliance.md`) and record the hand-off; if a §10 row is news to this plan set, add it as an `[OPEN]` item in the folder `plan.md` §10 for the TL.

## Outputs

- `docs/compliance/pdpd-memo-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md` (sections 1–12 above).
- §10 backlog rows handed to their owning plans/folders (tracked to M3 per README §B8.1).
- Hand-offs: 13.4.1-T1's filing notes (the verification submission must not claim compliance; it may state "memo delivered, backlog in progress"); 13.5.2-T1's calendar row for the memo's follow-up and the policy-review cadence; 13.5.1-T1's evidence index (memo reference + sha256).
- `tools/ci/check-compliance-docs.py` covering the memo (and the 13.1.x-T2 review logs' disposition completeness).

## Acceptance checks

- **Given** the memo, **when** `check-compliance-docs.py` runs, **then** all four rules pass: no blank gap cell, every required change has a backlog row with an existing owner, the four instrument names are verbatim, and `Decree 13/2023/ND-CP` appears only as superseded.
- **Given** the instrument dates, **when** the memo's §1 is read, **then** they carry ⚠️ and the secondary-source note — a `✅` next to a date fails the checker (K16).
- **Given** a duty that requires a Privacy Policy rewrite, **when** §10 is written, **then** the row names the document, the version-bump path through `D6`, and the re-acceptance consequence when material (`D7`).
- **Given** the eight `D16` duty families (consent wording, notices, records of processing, impact assessment, cross-border transfer to OpenAI, breach notification, children, DPO/contact), **when** §3 is read, **then** each has at least one row.
- **Given** the memo has landed, **when** any user-facing text or audit answer is checked, **then** a compliance statement may cite a §10 row; **given** the memo has not landed, **then** the forbidden-claims grep is green only because no such claim exists anywhere in the folder's artifacts.
- **Given** a duty whose change conflicts with a P contract (e.g. an acceptance model change), **when** it is analysed, **then** it is raised as a CR against the owning plan — this task does not reopen D1–D5 (`T7`).
- **Given** the memo contains no personal data or secret, **when** the D14 redaction grep runs over it, **then** it is clean.

## Tests

- `tools/ci/check-compliance-docs.py` + `tests/ci/test_check_compliance_docs.py` (pytest, mirroring `tests/ci/test_check_legal_versions.py`): fixtures for each rule — a blank gap cell, an orphan required change, a missing instrument name, a promoted `✅` date, a superseded instrument cited as applicable, a forbidden claim → each red; the real memo → green; `--selftest` green.
- No new domain test: the memo is a document, and its correctness is a legal judgement, not an assertion. What **is** asserted is that no artifact in the folder makes a claim the memo has not earned (the forbidden-claims grep, run over this folder's text artifacts).
- Manual (recorded): the owner reads the memo and confirms each §10 row's owning plan; the redaction grep result is recorded for 13.5.1-T1.
- No fake or mock: plain file parsing; no `NightTales.TestKit` type is needed.

## Failure handling and edge cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| The consultant's scope or fee is not settled | deliver a partial memo with §2 and §11 stating exactly what was covered, and §12 listing the uncovered questions as ⚠️ `[OPEN]` — a partial memo never reads as a clearance |
| The consultant cannot confirm a duty from the instruments | the duty stays ⚠️ with the reason (ambiguity, missing guidance) and the product change is **not** made on a guess; the row names what would settle it |
| A duty requires a product change with no owning plan | open a backlog row and add the `[OPEN]` item to the folder `plan.md` §10 for the TL, naming the candidate plan — do not create a new task id in this folder |
| A required change contradicts a signed P contract | raise a CR against the owning plan (e.g. plan 13 §2/§10 like CR-9); the memo records the conflict rather than a workaround that would reopen D1–D5 (`T7`) |
| The memo finds a **published** text wrong | §10 carries "re-issue <doc> at the next version"; the publish goes through `D6`; if material, re-acceptance through 13.2.1-T2/`D7`; until then nothing in the text may be relied on — mark the item as urgent, not as a pending nicety |
| The engagement slips past M2 | the memo is still delivered with what is settled; the calendar (13.5.2-T1) carries the follow-up row; no filing claims verification of the local law |
| The consultant is the same person as the 13.1.x-T2 reviewer | allowed and efficient (one engagement can cover both, exactly as 13.1.1-T2 and 13.1.2-T2 share one fee); the memo still records the fee scope separately from the document review — the cost lines must not be double-counted |
| A duty applies to a data class the platform does not hold | recorded as "not applicable — platform holds none", with the evidence (schema/flow reference), so the reader sees it was considered on purpose |
| The OAuth/YouTube filing asks a Vietnamese-law question | the answer cites the memo or states ⚠️; the filing must never assert compliance (`D16`) |

## Notes

- `D16` is the whole contract: instruments verbatim, obligations ⚠️ until confirmed, "what is not met yet" stated plainly, backlog rows owned by other plans — and the memo as the **only** gateway to a compliance claim.
- K16's trap is explicit: do not promote the 2025-06-26 / 2026-01-01 dates to ✅, and do not let a draft chase the repealed **Decree 13/2023/ND-CP** (`F17`).
- 13 §9's exit criteria close the memo's items by **M3**; README §B8.1 forbids creating an M3 task here — the items land in the owning plans.
- External consultant time is a cost line, not dev days (`O4`, README §B9); 0.5 d here is the analyst/day of work, not the fee.
- `D18` reuse: no new mechanism is introduced for breach notification, records of processing or contact — the memo attaches duties to the existing incident runbook, audit trail and pages.
- Sequencing note: because a memo-driven text change should land *with or before* the 13.1.x-T2 publish, the memo is worth starting early in M2 even though its canonical edge into 13.2.2-T1 is only soft.
