# Story rubric (D16) — 02.1.3 model A/B

Seven dimensions, 1–5 each. `factual_care` is `n/a` outside true crime and is excluded from those means (never scored 0). The same seven keys are `review.v1`'s `scores` object (D16), so this file is also the wording source for 02.6.1.

**`hook`** — 1: states a situation but gives nothing to wonder about; a listener would not stay. 3: raises a clear question within the first two sentences. 5: a concrete image plus an unresolved tension makes the next sentence unavoidable, in ≤ 2 sentences.

**`pacing`** — 1: uniform sentence length, the middle sags, the twist arrives without build-up. 3: beats in order (setup → build-up → clue → twist → reveal → aftermath) with at least one change of tempo. 5: every beat earns its place, tension rises, the reveal lands at the natural peak, aftermath ≤ 3 sentences.

**`atmosphere`** (without gore) — 1: fear is named ("it was scary") or bought with explicit gore. 3: atmosphere comes from concrete sensory detail; no explicit gore, no sexual content, no minor shown in danger (02 T73). 5: dread is built from the ordinary (sound, light, absence); the listener supplies the fear; nothing explicit is needed.

**`natural_language`** — 1: reads as translated English — wrong collocations, calques, stiff connectives; vi misuses or lacks diacritics. 3: native-sounding with occasional phrasing a native would not write. 5: reads as a native narrator writing for the ear — short spoken sentences, no brackets/lists/footnotes, numbers and dates said the way they are read aloud, one idea per sentence in tense moments.

**`originality`** — 1: a recognizable retelling of a known story; names, places or the twist match a well-known source. 3: familiar genre furniture, with at least one element (setting, mechanism or twist) that is not the default. 5: premise, mechanism and twist are each recognisably ours; no borrowed scene.

**`factual_care`** (true crime only; otherwise `n/a`) — 1: invented "facts" presented as true, or a real person/incident present. 3: claims are synthetic or match supplied material; no real person named. 5: every checkable claim is supplied or explicitly framed as unverified; no real-person and no real-incident signal, and internally consistent. ⚠️ M0 supplies no fact sheet (02.5.x is M1), so 5 here means "no real-person/real-incident signal + internally consistent", not source fidelity — accepted by T25, with `factual_care` excluded from the D15 comparison.

**`length_fit`** — 1: more than 15 % outside the tier token band, or the ending is cut off/padded. 3: within ±15 % of the band and the ending resolves. 5: inside the band and the story uses the room without a padded middle.
