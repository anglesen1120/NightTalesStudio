The lighthouse keeper counted seven lamps burning along the cliff road at dusk. Each one belonged to a family that had left the village years ago, yet the fires still answered the sea. He walked his lantern from the harbour to the tower, listening for the bell that only rang when fog rolled in. Tonight the fog came early, and the bell stayed quiet. He opened the logbook, wrote the hour twice, then twice more, watching ink dry slowly. By morning the lamps were out, and a single boat waited below, rocking gently in the calm water, holding one folded map with no destination written on it.
