The rain fell hard on the harbour road that night, and the old ferryman waited under the broken lamp.
A woman in a grey coat stepped out of the fog, holding a lantern that gave no warmth at all.
She asked him to carry her across the river before the bell in the chapel tower rang twelve times again.
He remembered the night the river turned silver and every lantern on the far bank went dark at once without a sound.
So he pushed the boat from the shore, and he did not look back until the bell went quiet.
