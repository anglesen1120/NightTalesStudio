The storm had swallowed the village by midnight, and the old lighthouse keeper sat alone in the dark. Somewhere below, footsteps brushed the iron stairs, slow, steady, closer. "Is anyone there?" he whispered. The radio crackled once, then answered in a voice he had buried thirty years ago. The door swung open without a creak. Nothing stood in the doorway but the smell of salt and rain, and a child's wet footprints crossing the floor.
