The old town is quiet after midnight, and a single lantern glows above the stone bridge. Narrow streets curve past wooden doors, and the cobblestones still hold the warmth of the day. A gray cat sits on a windowsill and watches the empty square with calm yellow eyes. Somewhere near the river, a bell in the clock tower rings once and the sound fades over the rooftops. The walker passes a closed bakery, where the smell of fresh bread drifts through a small open window. At the end of the lane, the moon rises over the hills and paints the chimneys silver.
